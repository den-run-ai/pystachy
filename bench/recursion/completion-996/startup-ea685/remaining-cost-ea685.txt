PR #996: remaining cost assessment at ea685

Conclusion
----------
The retained evidence does not identify another proven, low-risk source change
ready for adoption. The dominant remaining JIT tool-work difference is LLVM
machine-code generation. The separate AOT fnmatch loop regression remains
unexplained by the available profiles. Performance acceptance therefore remains
an explicit owner decision; the earlier rejection of 15% is not a new budget,
and none of the results below establishes an approved threshold.

This is a read-only analysis of existing records. No new compilation, benchmark,
profile, source change, or timing sample was performed for this note.

AOT fnmatch remains unresolved
------------------------------
The original six n=1000 AOT pairs were all slower than main. The 10x/100x
follow-up used the same already-built, hash-bound executables, fresh CPython
checksums and six balanced compiler orders. It retained all 36 observations
separately from the original 108; it did not rebuild or discard outliers.

At n=100000, loop medians were main 2.551652 s, da84 2.724147 s and candidate
2.764386 s. Candidate/main was +8.3371% by ratio of medians and +8.8572% by median
within-round paired ratio; four of six candidate/main pairs were slower. Process
CPU medians were +8.9459%, so this evidence does not clear the concern as merely
short-loop timing noise. Candidate/da84 was +1.4771% by ratio of loop medians;
da84/main was already +6.7602%. Those comparisons locate the historical gap but
do not causally attribute it to a particular source change.

Implication for the next action
------------------------------
The narrow measured improvements (shared checked leaf bodies, scalar guard
lowering, and ordinary inlining of eligible check/enter guards) are already in
the candidate. Earlier raw experiments outlining @rp2 children and selecting
ORC lazy code generation did not establish a useful improvement and were
rejected. Those experiments are preserved in:
/workspace/scratch/a83500db0e17/final-startup/curated-8ca9-investigation.json
SHA256 213f75ae483891658fa96d6362d539f77cbc380062eef27285dff1be28bae3c7

Further clone sharing would require new ABI/lifetime proof and measurement;
blanket region caps have no accepted budget. Restoring AOT-only forced guard
inlining is merely an untested diagnostic hypothesis, not an evidenced fix.
There is no specific additional optimization that this retained evidence proves
safe and beneficial enough to adopt. An owner can explicitly accept the measured
remaining costs, or choose a new, bounded causal investigation of AOT fnmatch
and remaining machine-pass work. This note does not start such an investigation
or silently convert a correctness pass into performance acceptance.
