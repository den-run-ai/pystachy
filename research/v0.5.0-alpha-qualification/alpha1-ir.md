# Alpha.1 exact-source IR audit

Baseline `5f81cc2563d231501952104e991d858e670325ac` → published candidate `41cc2dde4705efcf03d9d910455ac72fd5da2312`.

**4,544 cases complete; 0 new rejections; 0 substantive diagnostic changes; all 61 substantive differences explained.** Raw oracle exit 1 records 344 differences: 4,200 raw-identical cases, 269 generated-home-only LLVM differences, 14 source-location-only diagnostic differences, 18 new acceptances and 43 substantive LLVM differences. Compiler-self and runtime IR are raw-identical between the two compilers.

The candidate binary came from the completed local `0d695f5a5df049b30d891bd5f43b05ed1e117352` bootstrap; its tree equals the published candidate tree `d41286e801afd97f1fb45553d96ca76ccc336583`. All three compiler stages and all three runtime stages were byte-identical; the installed audit binary matched stage 2. The v0.4.0 binary and its previous complete compiler-source inventory matched the preserved hashes. Identities, full source snapshots, corpus identities and post-run source/binary checks are retained in `a1-identity.json`, `v4-identity.json` and `a1-source-rechecked.json`.

Both compilers read their own exact source library in equal-length `home-v4`/`home-a1` directories. The unchanged repository oracle compares exit status, complete diagnostics and raw LLVM, with evidence retention added. Every case has status/messages and changed cases retain full LLVM/diffs. The case list includes the complete canonical differential/rejection corpus plus compiler/runtime, benchmark and IR cases; inventory order and coverage were verified after execution.

Auxiliary location comparison substitutes only recognized generated ImportError module locations and exact compiler-home sys.prefix/base_prefix strings; diagnostic substitutions change only reviewed source-location forms. All tracked inputs were checked not to contain the audit-home literals. Twenty additional shared-corpus path strings are byte-identical in both raw outputs and were kept unchanged, not normalized. Their explicit review is in `a1-home-location-review.json`. No unexplained path substitution is used.

Of the previously substantive cases, 43 complete old/new LLVM pairs are byte-identical to the prior independently explained audit, and their test sources are unchanged. The tuple-union-kinds case differs from its prior output only in the generated import-location string and corresponding string length. Remaining final-repair differences were inspected directly; five newly substantive super cases also received a separate read-only control-flow review. Evidence is recorded in `a1-historical-ir-recheck.json` and `super-ir-independent-review.md`.

The two super-init deviation fixtures describe bounded runtime limitations for dynamic replacement receivers and __class__ overrides. This report does not establish complete first-class super support, native execution correctness, Lean proofs or exact-main CI; those gates are separate.
