[7] (high) Calling back into Python from compiled code (the Pys→Py direction, here sys.stdout.write) can release the GIL. If another thread then enters the runtime, its collector scans with the first thread's stack bottom and the process segfaults. A per-thread bottom (__thread) plus registered 'suspended spans' around call-outs fixes it; this is the Boehm GC_do_blocking / OCaml blocking-section pattern.
